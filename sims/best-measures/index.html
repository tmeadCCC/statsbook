What you should notice

When the data are bell-shaped (symmetric), use the mean to measure center and the standard deviation to measure variation. When the data are skewed, the long tail pulls the mean and inflates the standard deviation, so use the median and the IQR, which are resistant to extreme observations. After each answer, the green line shows the mean and the red line shows the median: on a skewed histogram, the mean is pulled toward the tail.
