Confidence Interval Simulation

Goes with Chapter 9 Confidence Intervals

Each click takes a random sample from a normal population and builds a confidence interval for the population mean from it. Intervals that capture the population mean μ are green; intervals that miss it are red. Watch what percent of the intervals contain μ as they stack up.

What to notice

Every sample gives a different interval, because every sample has a different mean x̄. With 95% confidence, about 95% of the intervals contain the population mean μ and about 5% miss it. The confidence level describes the method: in the long run, that percent of intervals made this way capture μ. A larger sample size makes the intervals narrower, and a higher confidence level makes them wider.
