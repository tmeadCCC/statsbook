Correlation Whisperer Game

Goes with Section 4.1 Scatterplots and Correlation

Look at the scatterplot and guess the correlation r, a number from −1 to 1. If your guess is within 0.15 of the actual correlation (within 0.2 when the correlation is close to 0), you win the round. Win 5 rounds in a row to become a Correlation Whisperer.

What you should notice

The faint lines mark the mean of x and the mean of y. When most points fall in the upper-right and lower-left regions, the correlation is positive; when most fall in the upper-left and lower-right, it is negative. The more tightly the points hug a straight line, the closer r is to 1 or −1. When the points are spread evenly over all four regions, r is close to 0.
