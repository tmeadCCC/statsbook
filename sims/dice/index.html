What to notice

After only 10 rolls the empirical probabilities can be far from the theoretical ones. Keep rolling: after a few thousand rolls with one die they settle close to 1/6 ≈ 0.1667 for each face. This is the Law of Large Numbers. With two dice, a sum of 7 comes up most often, because 6 of the 36 outcomes add to 7.
