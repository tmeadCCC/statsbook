What you should notice

Compare the P-value with the significance level α. If the P-value is less than α, we reject H0 and there is enough evidence to support the claim in HA. If the P-value is not less than α, we fail to reject H0 and there is not enough evidence to support it. The same P-value can lead to different decisions: a P-value of 0.03 rejects H0 at α = 0.05 but not at α = 0.01. A hypothesis test never proves either hypothesis.
