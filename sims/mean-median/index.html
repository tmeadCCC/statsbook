What you should notice

Every time a value moves, the mean changes, because every data value is included in its calculation. Moving one value far out pulls the mean toward it: the mean is sensitive to extreme observations. The median only depends on the order of the data. If the middle value stays in the middle, the median does not change, no matter how far the smallest or largest value moves: the median is resistant to extreme observations. The median only changes when the middle value itself moves, or when a value crosses over the middle.
