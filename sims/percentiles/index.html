What you should notice

The kth percentile, Pk, has about k% of the data at or below it, on its left in the dot plot, and about (100 − k)% above it. The quartiles are three special percentiles that split the data into four groups of about 25% each: Q1 is the 25th percentile, Q2 is the 50th percentile (the median), and Q3 is the 75th percentile. With 100 data values, each dot is 1% of the data, so you can count dots.
