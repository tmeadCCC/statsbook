What to notice

In a few spins you might come out ahead, especially with a lucky straight-up win of $350. Over hundreds or thousands of bets, your total winnings follow the expected total down: about −$0.53 per bet, or about −$530 for 1,000 bets, whichever of the three bets you choose. Every bet has the same expected value, so the casino expects to make about $0.53 on each one.
