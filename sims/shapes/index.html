What to notice

A bell-shaped (normal) histogram has one peak in the middle and looks about the same on both sides. A uniform histogram has bars that are all about the same height. A skewed histogram has a long tail on one side, and the name tells you where the tail is: skewed left has its tail on the left, and skewed right has its tail on the right.
